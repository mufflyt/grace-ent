# STROBE Statement — Checklist for Cross-Sectional Studies

Manuscript: *Subspecialty and Geographic Determinants of New-Patient Appointment Access and Wait Times in Otolaryngology: A National Mystery-Caller Study.*

Reporting follows the STROBE guideline for observational studies (von Elm E, et al. *Ann Intern Med.* 2007;147:573-577). "Location" refers to the section of the manuscript where each item is addressed; page/line numbers to be added on the typeset proof.

| # | Item | Recommendation | Location in manuscript |
|---|------|----------------|------------------------|
| 1 | Title & abstract | (a) Design in title/abstract; (b) informative, balanced abstract | Title ("A National Mystery-Caller Study"); Abstract (Objective/Methods/Results/Conclusions) |
| 2 | Background/rationale | Scientific background and rationale | Introduction ¶1–3 |
| 3 | Objectives | Specific objectives / hypotheses | Introduction, final ¶ (two-part access/timeliness aims) |
| 4 | Study design | Present key design elements early | Methods → Study design and sample ("cross-sectional, simulated-patient"); Rankin 2022 |
| 5 | Setting | Setting, locations, relevant dates | Methods → Study design and sample (US, 48 states, calls 2026-05-29 to 2026-07-06) |

################ 4. TIMELINESS MISSINGNESS ################
  wait_days is observed for 426 of 731 complete calls -- structurally MNAR: it exists only when an appointment
  was offered (offered==1 for 436 ; wait recorded for 426 ).
  The two-part hurdle model (scripts/model_wait_days.R) handles this by
  modeling the offer stage separately, so wait-time estimates are NOT
  extrapolated to offices that never offered an appointment.

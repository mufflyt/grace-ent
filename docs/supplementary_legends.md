# Supplementary material — table and figure legends

Generated by `scripts/supplementary_tables.R` and `scripts/supplementary_figures.R`
(outputs in `model_output/supp/`).

## Supplementary tables

- **Table S1. Full access model.** All odds ratios (95% CI, p) from the
  mixed-effects logistic model of appointment offer, including every covariate
  and the caller (operator) nuisance terms. `S1_access_full_model.csv`
- **Table S2. Full timeliness model.** All incidence rate ratios from the
  mixed-effects negative binomial model of business-day wait. `S2_timeliness_full_model.csv`
- **Table S3. Wait time by subspecialty.** Median (IQR) and n. `S3_wait_by_subspecialty.csv`
- **Table S4. Wait time by rurality.** `S4_wait_by_rurality.csv`
- **Table S5. Wait time by AAO-HNS region.** `S5_wait_by_region.csv`
- **Table S6. Offer-rate disparities by AAO-HNS region.** Offer rate, absolute
  difference, and relative risk (95% CI) vs the reference region. `S6_offer_disparities_by_region.csv`
- **Table S7. Access vs timeliness, side by side.** `S7_access_vs_timeliness.csv`
- **`supplemental_bundle.xlsx`** — the above bundled by `mysterycall_supplemental_tables`.

## Supplementary figures

- **Figure S1. Time to secured appointment, by subspecialty.** Kaplan–Meier
  curves showing the probability that a new-patient appointment had not yet been
  secured over 90 business days, for general otolaryngology, pediatric
  otolaryngology, laryngology, and pooled other subspecialties. Offices that
  never offered an appointment are censored at the 90-day horizon; a risk table
  and log-rank p are shown. `figS1_km_time_to_appointment.png`
- **Figure S2. Wait-time distribution by subspecialty.** Faceted histograms of
  business days to appointment among offering offices, illustrating the heavy
  right skew that motivates the negative binomial model. `figS2_wait_distribution.png`
- **Figure S3. Negative binomial model diagnostics.** DHARMa simulation-based
  residual diagnostics (uniform QQ and residual-vs-predicted) for the timeliness
  model. `figS3_nb_diagnostics.png`
- **Figure S4. Geographic variation by AAO-HNS region.** Choropleths of (a) the
  appointment offer rate and (b) the median business-day wait, mapped by AAO-HNS
  Board of Governors region. Descriptive; regional differences were not
  significant after adjustment. `figS4_choropleth_offer_rate.png`,
  `figS4_choropleth_median_wait.png`

> Note: `mysterycall`'s map layer delegates to the Google-based `mysterymaps`
> package; Figure S4 was therefore rendered directly (ggplot2 + `maps`) from the
> `data/raw/state_to_aao_hns_district.csv` crosswalk.
